Healing from a Painful Past

Many men have grown up in environments with little love, often leading to low self-esteem and a belief that they are unworthy of love. Negative experiences—such as parental neglect, emotional unavailability, or abuse—can deeply affect one's self-image.

Challenges Faced:

  • Parental Neglect: Lack of consistent affection can lead to feelings of abandonment.
  • Abuse and Criticism: Constant negative messaging fosters internalized unworthiness.
  • Lack of Positive Role Models: Without healthy examples, it’s hard to know what genuine love looks like.

Yet, through the love of Christ—expressed in Ephesians 2:4-5 ("But because of his great love for us, God, who is rich in mercy, made us alive with Christ even when we were dead in transgressions")—men can begin the journey of healing. Embracing therapy, community support, and spiritual guidance paves the way to rediscovering that your worth comes from being created and redeemed by God.
