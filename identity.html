What is Identity?

Identity is our understanding of who we are at our core. For Christian men, identity is defined by our relationship with God and is rooted in the truth that we are created in His image (Genesis 1:27). Our identity is not determined by external achievements or cultural standards but by being a beloved child of God.

What Does a Strong Sense of Identity Look Like?

  • Grounded in Truth: Rooted in Scripture, such as 2 Corinthians 5:17 and Psalm 139.
  • Self-Awareness and Clarity: Knowing your strengths, weaknesses, and purpose.
  • Resilience: Withstanding cultural pressures and external validation.
  • Authenticity: Living consistently with your values and beliefs.
