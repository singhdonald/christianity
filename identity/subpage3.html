Our Responsibility: Dying to Self & Embracing New Life in Christ

1. Dying to Self

Biblical Foundation:

"Then Jesus told His disciples, 'If anyone would come after Me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow Me.'" (Matthew 16:24)

Bonhoeffer’s quote echoes Jesus’ call to deny ourselves, which means letting go of our selfish desires, ambitions, and sinful nature.

Meaning: To follow Christ, we must "die" to our old way of life—our pride, self-reliance, and worldly pursuits. This is a daily process of surrendering our will to God’s.

2. Sacrificial Living

Biblical Foundation:

"I have been crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me." (Galatians 2:20)

Bonhoeffer emphasizes that answering Christ’s call requires sacrifice. It’s not a comfortable or easy path but one that demands giving up everything for the sake of the Gospel.

Meaning: This "death" is not physical but spiritual—a willingness to lay down our lives, our plans, and our comforts for the sake of Christ and His kingdom.

3. Radical Discipleship

Biblical Foundation:

"Whoever loves his life loses it, and whoever hates his life in this world will keep it for eternal life." (John 12:25)

Bonhoeffer, a German pastor and theologian, lived out this principle during World War II. He resisted the Nazi regime and was ultimately executed for his faith, embodying the cost of discipleship.

Meaning: Following Christ is not a half-hearted commitment. It requires total devotion, even if it leads to suffering, persecution, or death.

4. New Life in Christ

Biblical Foundation:

"For if we have been united with Him in a death like His, we shall certainly be united with Him in a resurrection like His." (Romans 6:5)

While the call is to "die," it is also a call to new life. Death to self leads to resurrection living—a life transformed by Christ’s power and presence.

Meaning: When we die to ourselves, we make room for Christ to live in and through us. This is where true freedom, joy, and purpose are found.

5. A Countercultural Call

Biblical Foundation:

"Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind." (Romans 12:2)

In a world that values self-promotion, comfort, and materialism, Bonhoeffer’s teaching challenges us to live differently.

Meaning: The call to die to self is countercultural. It goes against societal norms and calls us to live for something greater than ourselves—the glory of God.
