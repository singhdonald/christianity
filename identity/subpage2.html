Key Aspects of a Man’s Identity in Christ

  1. A Child of God:

    We are loved, chosen, and accepted by God (Ephesians 1:4-6).

  2. A Servant and Leader:

    Called to lead with humility and serve others as Christ did (Mark 10:45).

  3. A Warrior for God’s Kingdom:

    Equipped with spiritual armor to fight against sin and darkness (Ephesians 6:10-18).

  4. An Ambassador for Christ:

    Representing Christ in our words, actions, and relationships (2 Corinthians 5:20).

  5. A Friend of Jesus:

    Jesus calls us friends, not just servants (John 15:15).
